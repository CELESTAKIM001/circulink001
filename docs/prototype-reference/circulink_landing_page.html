How Circulink Works

A simple 3-step process to turn community waste into clean income

1

Separate & Schedule

Sort your recyclable waste at home or business and set a convenient pickup time online.

2

Smart Collection

Our trained youth collectors arrive, weigh your sorted items, and verify material quality.

3

Instant Cash Payout

Receive cash directly through M-PESA while materials go directly to certified industrial recyclers.
