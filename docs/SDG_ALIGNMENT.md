# CIRCULINK SDG alignment

CIRCULINK is designed to contribute to selected Sustainable Development Goals rather than claim ownership of those goals.

- SDG 8: livelihoods, productive activity and entrepreneurship around recovery and reuse.
- SDG 9: digital marketplace infrastructure and innovation.
- SDG 11: local collection networks and more sustainable urban material flows.
- SDG 12: prevention, reduction, recycling and reuse.
- SDG 13: potential emissions reduction through reuse and resource efficiency; impact must be measured rather than assumed.
- SDG 14: reduced leakage of waste into waterways is a potential impact pathway.
- SDG 15: improved material recovery can reduce pressure on land resources.
- SDG 17: partnerships among individuals, businesses, institutions and collection networks.

Source: United Nations, 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.
