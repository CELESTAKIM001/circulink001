# Paper and water resource note

Paper and pulp water use is highly process-specific. A 2023 Journal of Water Process Engineering article reports that pulp and paper production can consume about 10–300 m³ of water per tonne, with substantial variation by raw material, paper grade, process and region. It also notes that recycled-fibre mills generally use less water than virgin-fibre mills.

CIRCULINK should therefore describe recovered paper as a resource-efficiency opportunity, not claim a universal water-saving figure for every transaction.

Reference: Esmaeeli & Sarrafzadeh (2023), Reducing freshwater consumption in pulp and paper industries using pinch analysis and mathematical optimization.
